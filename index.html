About Us

One studio, every visual craft.

Creative Canvas Studio brings photography, videography, drone work and editing under one roof, so clients get a consistent look across every deliverable instead of piecing it together from different vendors.

We're based in Sri Lanka and shoot weddings, events, and commercial projects island-wide — including Colombo, Kandy, and Galle — as Sri Lanka's first all-in-one online booking studio.

Every project runs through the same standard: creative work, a professional team, genuine passion for quality, and delivery on time — every time.
