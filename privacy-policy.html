Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Creative Canvas Studio ("we", "us") operates this website. This page explains what information we collect from visitors and account holders, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and profile photo (if you sign in with Google or Facebook)
  • Reviews you submit: your name, star rating, comment, and any screenshot you choose to upload
  • Order details we add on your behalf: project title, timeline, and payment amounts, linked to your account email

How We Use Your Information

  • To let you sign in and manage your account
  • To display your reviews publicly on this website
  • To show you the status and payment details of your own orders
  • To communicate with you about your bookings via WhatsApp or email

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication and data storage, and may offer sign-in via Google or Facebook. When you use these sign-in options, those providers share your name, email, and profile photo with us, subject to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except the service providers (Firebase/Google) that power this website's login and storage features.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account, reviews, and order data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see our Data Deletion page for instructions.
